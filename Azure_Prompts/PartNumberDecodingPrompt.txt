PARAMETERS TO EXTRACT:

? ResistaOhm: Resistance value in Ohms (numeric only)
  - From: Part number legend OR ordering table
  - Example: "200", "1000", "10000"
  - Convert k/M to base units: "10k" ? 10000

? TolerancePct: Tolerance percentage (numeric only, without ±)
  - From: Part number legend OR specifications table
  - Example: "0.1", "0.5", "1", "5"

? PowerW: Power rating in Watts (numeric only)
  - From: Part number legend OR "Power Rating" specification
  - Example: "0.063", "0.1", "0.125", "0.25"

? PackageType: Package/case size
  - From: Part number legend OR package section
  - Example: "0201", "0402", "0603", "0805", "1206"

? TempCoeffPPM: Temperature coefficient in ppm/°C (numeric only)
  - From: "TCR" or "Temperature Coefficient" specification
  - Example: "25", "50", "100"
  - Look for: "±50 ppm/°C" ? extract "50"

? MaxOperatingTemp: Maximum operating temperature
  - From: "Operating Temperature Range" specification
  - Example: "155°C", "175°C"
  - Look for: "-55°C to +155°C" ? extract "155°C"

? MaxOverloadVoltage: Maximum overload voltage
  - From: "Maximum Overload Voltage" or "Continuous Working Voltage"
  - Example: "75V", "100V"
  - Look for: "Overload Voltage: 100V" ? extract "100"
