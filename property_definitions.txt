# Property Definitions / Erklärungen
# Diese Datei definiert, was einzelne Properties bedeuten
# Format: PropertyName=Erklärung auf Deutsch und Englisch

# 
# GENERAL PROPERTIES (all components)
# 

Function= The main function/purpose of the component (e.g. "Amplifier", "Filter", "Switch").  FOR RF AMPLIFIER: There are EXACTLY 3 allowed values - it MUST be one of: (1) "Radio Frequency Amplifier", (2) "Broadband Active Balun", (3) "RF PA Linearizer". NO other values allowed!  FOR RESISTOR CONSTANT: There are EXACTLY 5 allowed values - it MUST be one of: (1) "THICK FILM", (2) "THIN FILM", (3) "METAL FOIL", (4) "WIRE-WOUND", (5) "CARBON FILM". LOOK IN: "Features", "Description", "Technology" section. KEYWORDS: "Thick Film", "Thin Film", "Metal Foil", "Wire Wound", "Wirewound", "Carbon Film". ⚠️⚠️⚠️ CRITICAL FOR RESISTOR: If "Function" field says "nicht vorhanden", CHECK DESCRIPTION! Often the technology is mentioned there (e.g. "Metal Film (Thin Film)" → Function = "THIN FILM"). EXAMPLES: "RE series thick film chip resistor" → "THICK FILM", "Metal Film (Thin Film) Chip Resistor" → "THIN FILM", "Precision thin film resistor" → "THIN FILM", "Wire-wound power resistor" → "WIRE-WOUND". ⚠️ IMPORTANT: Use EXACT capitalization as shown above!

Description= Short text description of the component. LOOK IN: Title, "Description" section, "Features" section

PartNumber=Manufacturer part number / order number. LOOK IN: Title page, "Ordering Information" table, "Part Number" section. IMPORTANT: Series datasheets may have multiple part numbers!

Manufacturer= Name of the manufacturer. LOOK IN: Title page, logo, "Manufacturer" field. COMMON MANUFACTURERS: Analog Devices, Texas Instruments, Infineon, STMicroelectronics, NXP, Rohde & Schwarz

PackageType=Package type. LOOK IN: "Package Information", "Mechanical Specifications", "Ordering Information". COMMON TYPES: QFN, QFP, SOIC, SOT-23, BGA, DIP, TO-220

# 
# MECHANICAL PROPERTIES
# 

NumberOfPin=Number of connection pins. LOOK IN: "Package Information", "Pin Configuration", pin diagram. VALIDATION: Must be integer (2-1000). ERROR SOURCE: Don't confuse with "Pin Width" or "Package Size"

Pitch=Distance between two pins/contacts in mm or um. LOOK IN: "Package Dimensions", "Mechanical Drawings", "Package Outline". KEYWORDS: "Pin Pitch", "Lead Pitch", "Pin Spacing", "e=", "pitch". ERROR SOURCES: Don't confuse with "Pin Width", "Body Size" or "Lead Length". If mechanical drawing available: Measure distance between pin centers

LengthMm=Length in millimeters. LOOK IN: "Package Dimensions", "Mechanical Outline". FORMAT: Often as "L x W x H" or "Length x Width x Height". VALIDATION: 0.5mm to 500mm

WidthMm=Width in millimeters. LOOK IN: "Package Dimensions", "Mechanical Outline". VALIDATION: 0.5mm to 500mm

HeiMm=Height in millimeters. LOOK IN: "Package Dimensions", "Mechanical Outline", "Height" or "Thickness". VALIDATION: 0.1mm to 50mm

DiameterMm=Diameter in millimeters. LOOK IN: "Mechanical Dimensions", "Package Outline". APPLICABLE: Round components (capacitors, inductors, connectors)

InnerDia=Inner diameter in mm. LOOK IN: "Mechanical Dimensions". APPLICABLE: Hollow cylinders, bushings, sleeves

OuterDiam=Outer diameter in mm. LOOK IN: "Mechanical Dimensions". APPLICABLE: Cables, connectors, cylindrical housings

Thickness=Thickness/wall thickness in mm. LOOK IN: "Mechanical Dimensions", "Material Thickness"

#
# ELECTRICAL PROPERTIES (RF/HF-components)
#

FMinHz=Minimum operating frequency in Hz. LOOK IN: "Electrical Specifications", "Frequency Range", "Operating Frequency". KEYWORDS: "Minimum Frequency", "f_min", "Lower Frequency", "DC to ...". ⚠️⚠️⚠️ CRITICAL DC HANDLING: If datasheet says "DC to X MHz" or "DC coupled", extract FMinHz as "0 Hz" (NOT "DC"!). DC = Direct Current = 0 Hz frequency. VALIDATION: 0 Hz to 1 THz. UNITS: Hz, kHz, MHz, GHz. EXAMPLES: "DC to 1000 MHz" → FMinHz = "0 Hz", "50 MHz to 1 GHz" → FMinHz = "50 MHz"

FMaxHz=Maximum operating frequency in Hz. LOOK IN: "Electrical Specifications", "Frequency Range". KEYWORDS: "Maximum Frequency", "f_max", "Upper Frequency". VALIDATION: 0 Hz to 1 THz. ⚠️ IMPORTANT: This frequency is the REFERENCE for GainTypDb, Op1DbDbm and Oip3TypDbm!

FNomTypHz=Nominal/typical frequency in Hz. LOOK IN: "Electrical Specifications", "Nominal Frequency". APPLICABLE: Oscillators, filters, resonators

GainTypDb=Typical gain in dB (Small Signal Gain). LOOK IN: "Electrical Specifications" table, "Performance Characteristics". ⚠️⚠️⚠️ CRITICAL - FOLLOW THESE RULES: (1) ALWAYS READ AT MAXIMUM FREQUENCY (FMaxHz) - This is the "worst-case" value!, (2) When multiple frequencies in table: Take value at HIGHEST frequency, (3) Do NOT confuse with "Large Signal Gain", "Gain at P1dB" or "Power Gain", (4) If only one frequency point: Verify it's FMaxHz. VALIDATION: -10 dB to +50 dB. COMPONENT TYPE HINTS: RF Amplifier=10-30dB (at FMax!), OP Amplifier=60-140dB (Open Loop), LNA=15-25dB (at FMax!)

GbwTypHz=Gain-bandwidth product in Hz. LOOK IN: "AC Performance", "Frequency Response". KEYWORDS: "GBW", "GBWP", "Gain-Bandwidth Product", "Unity-Gain Bandwidth". APPLICABLE: Operational amplifiers. VALIDATION: 1 kHz to 10 GHz

NfTypDb=Noise figure in dB - measures internal noise. LOOK IN: "Electrical Specifications", "Noise Performance". KEYWORDS: "Noise Figure", "NF", "Noise Factor". ⚠️ IMPORTANT: (1) Lower values are better!, (2) For multiple frequencies: Take value at maximum frequency (FMaxHz). VALIDATION: 0.1 dB to 10 dB. TYPICAL VALUES: LNA=0.5-2dB, Mixer=8-15dB

Op1DbDbm=Output 1dB compression point in dBm. LOOK IN: "Electrical Specifications", "Linearity". KEYWORDS: "Output P1dB", "OP1dB", "1dB Compression Point", "Output 1dB Compression". ⚠️⚠️⚠️ CRITICAL - REFERENCE FREQUENCY: (1) ✅ ALWAYS READ AT MAXIMUM FREQUENCY (FMaxHz) - This is the "worst-case" value!, (2) When multiple frequencies in table: Take value at HIGHEST frequency, (3) If multiple columns (e.g. different gain settings): Take "Typical" or standard setting, (4) Do NOT confuse with "Input P1dB" or "P1dB" without "Output". VALIDATION: -20 dBm to +40 dBm

RfinMaxDbm= Maximum RF input power in dBm. LOOK IN: "Absolute Maximum Ratings", "Input Power". KEYWORDS: "Maximum Input Power", "RF Input Power", "P_in max". VALIDATION: -30 dBm to +30 dBm

Oip3TypDbm=Output third-order intercept point in dBm. LOOK IN: "Electrical Specifications", "Linearity", "Intermodulation". KEYWORDS: "OIP3", "Output IP3", "Third-Order Intercept", "Output Third Order Intercept". ⚠️⚠️⚠️ CRITICAL - REFERENCE FREQUENCY: (1) ✅ ALWAYS READ AT MAXIMUM FREQUENCY (FMaxHz) - This is the "worst-case" value!, (2) When multiple frequencies in table: Take value at HIGHEST frequency, (3) "OIP3" is frequency-dependent and degrades at high frequencies, (4) Do NOT confuse with "IIP3" (Input IP3) - we need OUTPUT!, (5) If only "IP3" without "Output/Input": Check context - usually means Output. VALIDATION: -10 dBm to +50 dBm. FORMULA: OIP3 ≈ OP1dB + 10dB (rough rule)

RfinMaxOhm=Maximum input resistance in Ohms. LOOK IN: "Input Characteristics", "Impedance". VALIDATION: 0.1 Ohm to 10 MOhm

Impedance=Characteristic impedance. LOOK IN: "RF Characteristics", "Input/Output Impedance". COMMON VALUES: 50 Ohm (RF), 75 Ohm (Video/TV), 100 Ohm (Differential), 600 Ohm (Audio). VALIDATION: 1 Ohm to 1000 Ohm
